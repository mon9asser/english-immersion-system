
| Idiom                             | Meaning                                                         | Example Sentence                                  |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| for the record                    | To make something clear or officially state something           | For the record, I support gender equality.        |
| stand out                         | To be noticeable or more important than others                  | Her speech stood out from the others.             |
| take up the mantle                | To accept responsibility for a cause or role                    | I want to take up the mantle and help others.     |
| step forward                      | To volunteer or take action                                     | We need more people to step forward.              |
| speak up                          | To express your opinion openly                                  | You should speak up when something is unfair.     |
| in fact                           | Used to emphasize or clarify a statement                        | In fact, many people agree with this idea.        |
| to date                           | Until now; up to the present time                               | To date, we haven't solved the problem.           |
| in doing so                       | As a result of doing that action                                | In doing so, we can make a difference.            |
| if not me, who? If not now, when? | A call to take responsibility and act immediately               | If not me, who? If not now, when?                 |
| roll up your sleeves              | To prepare to work hard (related idiom, not used in the speech) | Let's roll up our sleeves and finish the project. |
