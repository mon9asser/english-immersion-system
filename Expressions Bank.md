## Story 1:  The Unexpected Shortcut

| Expression              | Meaning                                    | Example Sentence                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Come down in buckets    | Rain very heavily                          | It's coming down in buckets today.    |
| Run errands             | Go out to complete small tasks             | I need to run errands this afternoon. |
| Run behind              | Be late or behind schedule                 | I'm running behind today.             |
| Pick up                 | Collect something or someone               | I'll pick up the cake.                |
| Drop off                | Take something or someone to a place       | I'll drop off the package.            |
| Pop into someone's head | Suddenly come to mind                      | A great idea popped into my head.     |
| Turn onto               | Move onto a different road                 | Turn onto the next street.            |
| Make good time          | Travel or progress quickly                 | We made good time on the highway.     |
| Let out a sigh          | Breathe out audibly to show emotion        | She let out a long sigh.              |
| Break down              | Stop working, especially a vehicle         | My car broke down yesterday.          |
| Break something down    | Explain something in simpler parts         | Let's break down this expression.     |
| Read out loud           | Read using your voice                      | I read the story out loud every day.  |
| Dive into               | Start exploring something enthusiastically | Let's dive into today's lesson.       |
