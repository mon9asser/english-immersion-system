## Story 1:  The Unexpected Shortcut

| Idiom                  | Meaning                                         | Example Sentence                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| In a jam               | In a difficult situation                        | I'm in a jam because I lost my keys.           |
| In a pickle            | In a tricky or difficult situation              | We're in a pickle because we missed the train. |
| Roll with the punches  | Adapt to difficulties without getting too upset | Plans changed, but I'll roll with the punches. |
| This is just my luck!  | Said when something unlucky happens             | I missed the bus. This is just my luck!        |
| Stuck in traffic       | Unable to move freely because of heavy traffic  | I'm stuck in traffic right now.                |
| Not a car in sight     | No cars can be seen anywhere                    | There wasn't a car in sight. not a car or another soul in sight                  |
| Get out of a situation | Find a way to escape or solve a problem         | I need to get out of this difficult situation. |
