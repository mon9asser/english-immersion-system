| Grammar Point               | Rule / Structure                                   | Example from the Story                                                                                           | Additional Example                                      |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Present simple              | Subject + base verb                                | We want to end gender inequality.                                                                                | I study English every day.                              |
| Present perfect             | Subject + have/has + past participle               | I have realized that fighting for women’s rights has too often become synonymous with man-hating.                | I have learned many new expressions.                    |
| Present perfect continuous  | Subject + have/has been + verb-ing                 | No direct example of this tense in the speech.                                                                   | I have been studying English for two years.             |
| Past simple                 | Subject + past verb                                | I decided I was a feminist.                                                                                      | I started learning Python last year.                    |
| Past perfect                | Subject + had + past participle                    | My parents didn’t love me less because I was born a daughter. (The sentence uses past simple, not past perfect.) | I had finished my work before the meeting started.      |
| When clauses                | When + subject + verb, subject + verb              | When, at 14, I started being sexualized by certain elements of the press.                                        | When I arrived home, I called my friend.                |
| Because clauses             | Main clause + because + reason                     | My life is a sheer privilege because my parents didn’t love me less because I was born a daughter.               | I practice speaking because I want to become fluent.    |
| Relative clauses            | Noun + who/that/which + clause                     | The inadvertent feminists who are changing the world today.                                                      | I know a developer who builds AI applications.          |
| Passive voice               | Subject + be + past participle                     | I was appointed six months ago.                                                                                  | The application was developed in Node.js.               |
| Modal verb: should          | Subject + should + base verb                       | Both men and women should feel free to be sensitive.                                                             | You should practice speaking aloud.                     |
| Modal verb: can             | Subject + can + base verb                          | We can all be freer.                                                                                             | You can improve your pronunciation with practice.       |
| First conditional           | If + present simple, will + base verb              | If we do nothing, it will take 75 years.                                                                         | If I practice daily, I will improve.                    |
| Second conditional          | If + past simple, would + base verb                | No direct example of the standard second conditional in the speech.                                              | If I had more time, I would study another hour.         |
| Negative conditional        | If + subject + don't/doesn't + verb, result clause | If men don’t have to be aggressive in order to be accepted, women won’t feel compelled to be submissive.         | If I don't practice, I won't improve.                   |
| Infinitive of purpose       | to + base verb                                     | We want to try and galvanize as many men and boys as possible.                                                   | I study English to communicate with clients.            |
| Gerund after a preposition  | Preposition + verb-ing                             | Thank you. (No gerund example in this closing phrase; see the additional example.)                               | I am interested in learning English.                    |
| Comparative structure       | more/less + adjective or adverb + than             | My parents didn’t love me less because I was born a daughter.                                                    | This exercise is more difficult than the last one.      |
| The more…, the more…        | The + comparative, the + comparative               | The more I have spoken about feminism, the more I have realized…                                                 | The more I practice, the more confident I become.       |
| Too + adjective             | too + adjective to express excess                  | Too strong, too aggressive…                                                                                      | This sentence is too complicated to understand quickly. |
| Not … but …                 | Contrasts two ideas                                | It is not the word that is important but the idea and the ambition behind it.                                    | It is not speed but consistency that matters.           |
| Both … and …                | Connects two equally important things              | Both men and women should feel free to be sensitive.                                                             | Both reading and listening help me learn English.       |
| Before + clause             | Before + subject + verb                            | Before women can expect to be paid the same as men for the same work.                                            | I review my notes before I go to bed.                   |
| Reported speech             | Reporting verb + reported statement                | Edmund Burke said: “All that is needed…”                                                                         | She said that she wanted to help.                       |
| Embedded question           | Question word + subject + verb                     | Why is the word such an uncomfortable one? (Direct question; compare the additional example.)                    | I don't know why the word is so controversial.          |
| Cleft sentence for emphasis | What + clause + be + emphasized information        | What stood out for me the most was that only 30 per cent of her audience were male.                              | What I need most is more speaking practice.             |
